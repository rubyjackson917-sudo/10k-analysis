10-K Analysis: Apple vs. Microsoft

Ten years of annual reports (10-Ks) turned into ratios, MD&A sentiment and stock prices. All amounts in US dollars.

Financial ratios

One ratio per category, calculated from the extracted financial statements.

Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities. Debt-to-equity = total liabilities / total shareholders' equity. Asset turnover = total revenue / year-end total assets. Net margin = net income / total revenue. Return on equity = net income / total shareholders' equity.

MD&A sentiment (Loughran-McDonald dictionary)

Word counts after cleaning the MD&A text. P/N ratio = positive words / negative words.

What the data shows

    Additional analysis: net income returned to shareholders

    What share of net income did Apple and Microsoft return to shareholders, and how did this change their shareholders' equity? Ten-year totals: Apple FY2016 to FY2025, Microsoft FY2017 to FY2026.

    Opening equity is from the prior-year column of each company's first 10-K.

    Apple returned $855.0 billion to shareholders, 112% of its $763.5 billion net income, and its equity fell from $119.4 billion to $73.7 billion. Microsoft returned $385.5 billion, 59% of its net income, and its equity grew about six times, from $72.0 billion to $442.4 billion. Large buybacks shrank Apple's equity, so the same net income looks like a much larger percentage. This is why Apple's return on equity (152%) is far higher than Microsoft's (30%), even though Microsoft has the higher net profit margin.

    Conclusion

    This analysis compared Apple and Microsoft over ten years using financial ratios, MD&A sentiment and stock prices. Based on the findings, Microsoft has the stronger financial position: its higher current ratio, lower debt and higher net profit margin leave it better able to meet its obligations. Apple's higher return on equity comes from debt and share buybacks rather than higher profitability. MD&A tone showed no relationship with either company's stock price, so financial ratios are a more reliable basis for comparison.

    Source code

    The Python programs that produced these results are in this repository, one file per stage: stage_a_download_10k.py (download), stage_b_extract_mdna.py (MD&A), stage_c_extract_financials.py (statements), stage_d_sentiment.py (Loughran-McDonald scoring), stage_e_stock_prices.py (prices) and stage_f_analysis.py (ratios, summary and chart).

    Source: SEC EDGAR 10-K filings, extracted with Python (download, MD&A and statement extraction, Loughran-McDonald scoring, Yahoo Finance prices). Figures are as originally reported; Apple's fiscal year ends in late September and Microsoft's on June 30; prices are split-adjusted, not dividend-adjusted. Correlations on ten points are suggestive only.
